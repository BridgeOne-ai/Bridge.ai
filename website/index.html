Feel better about how you use AI chatbots.

Bridge.ai is a Chrome extension that notices how you feel when you talk to ChatGPT and Gemini, catches personal information before you send it, and protects children from risky conversations. A model inside your browser does the reading, so nothing you write ever leaves your computer.

How it works

Understands meaning, not just keywords

Each message is read by Google's EmbeddingGemma, a small model that runs inside Chrome on your graphics card or processor. It downloads once and then works offline.

Feelings and interests

27 feelings, from stress and loneliness to pride and calm, and what you chat about: sports, studies, games, music and more.

Personal info guard

Phone numbers, emails, addresses, IDs and passwords are caught before sending, with one click to remove them. Card and Social Security numbers can never be sent.

Patterns over a week

A rolling 7-day score notices slow drifts, like relying on a chatbot in place of people, even when no single message is alarming.

Gentle nudges

A short card when use starts to look unhealthy, at most three a day: "An AI can listen, but it can't show up for you. Who in your life can?"

A private dashboard

Your week at a glance: feelings by hour and day, interests, time per chatbot, and what's behind the pattern score. It reads only this browser's own data.

Visible, never hidden

A small "Monitored by Bridge.ai" or "Protected by Bridge.ai" notice sits on every chat it covers. You can pause it any time.

Two modes

For you, or to protect a child

Chosen once at setup, and switchable later. Leaving Child mode needs the parent PIN.

For adults

Parent mode

  • See which feelings and interests come up in your AI chats
  • A 7-day pattern score with what's behind it
  • Personal info is paused: send without it, or send anyway
  • A warning before treating a chatbot as a friend or partner
  • Nothing is ever blocked; you decide
Set up by a parent

Child mode

  • Messages about self-harm, meeting strangers, sexual content or violence are held back before the chatbot gets them
  • Personal info can be sent with the details removed, or as typed once a parent approves with the PIN
  • The PIN is typed in Bridge.ai's own window, never on the chat site
  • A parent sees only counts of what was held back, never the child's words, feelings or topics
  • The child always sees "Protected by Bridge.ai": protection, not surveillance

Install

Install in about two minutes

Bridge.ai isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it's installed as an "unpacked" extension. Chrome on a computer only, not on phones.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download bridge-ai-extension.zip and unzip it. You'll get a folder called bridge-ai. Keep it somewhere permanent, like Documents: Chrome loads Bridge.ai from that folder.

    Download the latest version

  2. Open Chrome's extensions page and turn on Developer mode

    Type chrome://extensions in the address bar and press Enter. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

    Developer mode

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the bridge-ai folder from step 1.

    Load unpacked

  4. Finish setup

    Bridge.ai's setup page opens. Read what it reads and keeps, choose Parent or Child mode (Child mode asks you to create a parent PIN), and agree. Then pin Bridge.ai from the puzzle-piece menu in Chrome's toolbar and open ChatGPT or Gemini.

Updating: download the new zip, replace the old bridge-ai folder with the new one, then click the reload arrow on Bridge.ai's card in chrome://extensions. Your settings and PIN are kept. All versions and what changed.

Questions

Good to know

Which sites does it work on?

ChatGPT (chatgpt.com) and Gemini (gemini.google.com) get everything: feelings, interests, the personal info guard and Child-mode protection. On Claude and Character.AI it only counts time; messages aren't read. It doesn't read any other website.

Why does Chrome show a "Developer mode" warning?

Chrome shows it for every extension that isn't installed from the Chrome Web Store. It's expected for an unpacked install. You can read all of Bridge.ai's code on GitHub.

Can a child turn Child mode off?

Not from Bridge.ai itself: leaving Child mode, pausing it, or approving a held-back message needs the parent PIN. But a determined child could use an Incognito window, another browser, or remove the extension from chrome://extensions. For a locked-down setup, use Chrome's family controls (Google Family Link) alongside Bridge.ai.

Does it work offline? Is it slow?

After the one-time model download it works offline. Checks are usually quick, faster with a graphics card. Until the model has downloaded, built-in phrase checks run instead.

Does it work in other browsers or on phones?

It's built for Chrome on computers (Windows, Mac and Linux), and has been tested most on Mac. Other Chromium-based browsers may work but aren't tested. It doesn't run on phones or tablets.

Is it a safety or medical tool?

No. Bridge.ai is a supplement to talking with each other, not a safety guarantee, a medical tool or a crisis service. If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).
