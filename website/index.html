Feel better about how you use AI chatbots.

Bridge.ai is a Chrome extension that notices how you feel when you talk to ChatGPT and Gemini, catches personal information before you send it, and protects children from risky conversations. Nothing you write ever leaves your computer.

How it works

A small AI that stays on your computer

Bridge.ai reads each message with a model that runs inside Chrome. Your words are checked right there, and never sent to a server.

You type a messageon ChatGPT or Gemini, like always.

Bridge.ai checks it on your computerusing a small AI model that runs inside Chrome.

You get gentle insightfeelings, interests, and a pause if something looks personal.

Features

Everything it does for you

Thoughtful help in the background, never in the way.

Feelings and interests

Notices 27 feelings, from stress and loneliness to pride and calm, and what you love chatting about.

Personal info guard

Catches phone numbers, emails, addresses and passwords before you send them, with one click to remove them.

Patterns over a week

Spots slow changes, like leaning on a chatbot in place of friends, even when no single message stands out.

Gentle nudges

A friendly reminder when chatbot use starts to look unhealthy, like "Who in your life could you tell this to?"

A private dashboard

Your week at a glance: feelings by hour and day, interests, and time spent with each chatbot.

Always visible

A small "Bridge.ai" badge shows on every chat it covers, so it's never hidden. Pause it whenever you like.

Two modes

For you, or to protect a child

Pick one when you set it up. You can switch later; leaving Child mode needs the parent PIN.

For adults

Parent mode

  • See your own feelings and interests in AI chats
  • A weekly pattern score, and what's behind it
  • Personal info is paused: remove it, or send anyway
  • Nothing is ever blocked; you're in charge
Set up by a parent

Child mode

  • Risky messages are held back before the chatbot gets them
  • Personal info is removed, or sent once a parent approves with a PIN
  • A parent sees only what was held back, never the child's words
  • The child always sees that Bridge.ai is on

Install

Ready in about two minutes

Bridge.ai isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it takes four quick steps. Works with Chrome on a computer.

  1. Download and unzip

    Download the zip and unzip it. Keep the bridge-ai folder in Documents.

  2. Turn on Developer mode

    Go to chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode, top right.

  3. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the bridge-ai folder.

  4. Finish setup

    Bridge.ai opens its setup page. Choose Parent or Child mode, and you're done.

Questions

Good to know

Which sites does it work on?

ChatGPT and Gemini get everything: feelings, interests, the personal info guard and Child-mode protection. On Claude and Character.AI it only counts time. It doesn't read any other website.

Why does Chrome mention "Developer mode"?

Chrome asks for it for any extension that isn't from the Chrome Web Store. It's expected, and all of Bridge.ai's code is open on GitHub.

Can a child turn Child mode off?

Not from Bridge.ai: leaving Child mode, pausing it, or approving a held-back message needs the parent PIN. A determined child could still use Incognito, another browser, or remove the extension, so for full control use Google Family Link alongside it.

Does it work offline? Does it slow things down?

After a one-time download of the AI model it works offline, and checks are usually quick. Until the model has downloaded, simpler built-in checks run instead.

Does it work on phones or in other browsers?

It's made for Chrome on Windows, Mac and Linux computers. It doesn't run on phones or tablets.

Is it a safety or medical tool?

No. Bridge.ai is a supplement to talking with each other, not a safety guarantee or a medical tool. If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).
