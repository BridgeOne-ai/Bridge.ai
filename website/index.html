Download

Feel better about how you use AI chatbots.

Bridge.ai is a Chrome extension that notices how you feel when you talk to ChatGPT and Gemini, catches personal information before you send it, and protects children from risky conversations. It runs on your own computer, so nothing you write ever leaves it.

Download for Chrome Watch the demo

Free and open source. Chrome on a computer. No account needed.

What it does

Each message is read by a small AI model that runs inside Chrome. It downloads once and then works offline.

Feelings and interests

Notices 27 feelings, like stress, loneliness or pride, and what you chat about: sports, studies, games, music and more.

Personal info guard

Catches phone numbers, emails, addresses and passwords before you send them, with one click to remove them.

Patterns over a week

Notices slow changes, like relying on a chatbot in place of people.

Gentle nudges

A short reminder when chatbot use starts to look unhealthy. At most three a day.

A private dashboard

Your week at a glance: feelings, interests and time per chatbot.

Always visible

A small "Bridge.ai" notice shows on every chat it covers. You can pause it any time.

Two modes

Parent mode, for adults

  • See your own feelings and interests in AI chats
  • Personal info is paused: remove it, or send anyway
  • Nothing is ever blocked

Child mode, set up by a parent

  • Risky messages are held back before the chatbot gets them
  • Personal info is removed, or sent once a parent approves with a PIN
  • A parent sees only what was held back, never the child's messages

Privacy

Nothing you write leaves your computer. Bridge.ai has no server and no account.

  • Messages are checked in memory and never stored.
  • No analytics, ads or tracking.
  • Counts are kept on your computer for at most 14 days.
  • The only download is the AI model itself, once.

How to install

Bridge.ai isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet, so it takes a few extra steps. Chrome on a computer only.

  1. Download the zip and unzip it. Keep the bridge-ai folder somewhere permanent, like Documents.
  2. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and choose the bridge-ai folder.
  4. Bridge.ai's setup page opens. Choose Parent or Child mode and agree.

To update later, replace the folder with the new one and click the reload arrow on Bridge.ai in chrome://extensions.

Questions

Which sites does it work on?

ChatGPT and Gemini. On Claude and Character.AI it only counts time. It doesn't read any other website.

Why does Chrome mention "Developer mode"?

Chrome asks for it for any extension that isn't from the Chrome Web Store. All of Bridge.ai's code is on GitHub.

Can a child turn Child mode off?

Not from Bridge.ai: that needs the parent PIN. But a child could use Incognito, another browser, or remove the extension. For full control, use Google Family Link too.

Does it work on phones or other browsers?

It's made for Chrome on Windows, Mac and Linux computers. It doesn't run on phones.

Is it a safety or medical tool?

No. It's a supplement to talking with each other, not a safety guarantee. If you or someone you know is in crisis, contact local emergency services or a crisis line (in the US, call or text 988).
